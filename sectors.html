Sectors & Services

Technical details on our three core trade sectors — from regulatory compliance to freight modes and supply chain flows for B2B buyers across Europe and beyond.

Sector 01

Healthcare &
Medical Devices

Compliant, certified supply chains for medical devices, pharmaceuticals, and hospital consumables — sourced from India's leading manufacturers and delivered to European healthcare systems.

ISO 13485 CE Marking FDA Ready EU MDR 2017/745 CDSCO Approved

Medical Devices (Class I/II/III)

Surgical instruments, diagnostics, implants, and wearables — CE-marked and audit-ready for EU markets.

Pharmaceuticals & APIs

GMP-certified Active Pharmaceutical Ingredients and finished formulations with full dossier support.

Hospital Consumables

Gloves, masks, syringes, IV sets, and disposables — bulk supply to hospitals, clinics, and distributors.

Cold Chain & Sensitive Logistics

Temperature-controlled freight for biologics, vaccines, and pharmaceuticals requiring 2–8°C or -20°C conditions.

Regulatory Documentation

Technical files, Declaration of Conformity, CE certificates, FDA registrations, and customs clearance documentation.

Secure Supply Chain Management

Track-and-trace, anti-counterfeit measures, serialisation, and full audit trail from Indian factory to European hospital.

Regulatory Note

All medical device exports from India to the EU comply with EU MDR 2017/745 and EU IVDR 2017/746. We work with accredited EU Notified Bodies and maintain ISO 13485:2016 certified quality management systems. Documentation packages available upon request.

Sector 02

Agri-Food &
Beverages

From India's finest spices and basmati to organic pulses and specialty teas — we handle the complete export journey to European retail, wholesale, and food service networks.

FSSAI Certified EU Food Law Compliant Phytosanitary Certified Organic NOP/EU APEDA Registered

Spices & Condiments

Turmeric, cumin, cardamom, black pepper, coriander — whole, ground, and blended for retail and industrial buyers.

Basmati & Non-Basmati Rice

Premium long-grain basmati varieties and commodity rice for European supermarkets, ethnic food distributors, and HoReCa.

Pulses & Lentils

Chana, moong, toor, urad, and masoor dal — cleaned, graded, and packed to EU food-safety specifications.

Teas & Herbal Infusions

Darjeeling, Assam, and specialty single-estate teas alongside ayurvedic herbal blends for European wellness markets.

Processed & Packaged Foods

Ready-to-cook, snacks, pickles, chutneys, and Indian ethnic food brands packaged for European retail shelves.

Phytosanitary & Food Safety Compliance

Full documentation: FSSAI certificates, phytosanitary passes, lab test reports, COA/COO, and EU customs entry.

Sector 03

General Cargo &
Industrial Goods

From textiles and garments to machinery, auto components, and construction materials — we manage B2B freight flows across India-Europe trade corridors with full customs handling.

Customs Licensed IATA Accredited Dangerous Goods Certified

Textiles, Garments & Fabrics

Cotton, silk, technical fabrics, and ready-made garments — export packaging, inspection, and EU customs classification.

Machinery & Auto Components

Industrial machinery, auto parts, engineering goods — heavy lift coordination, OOG cargo, and customs clearance across EU ports.

Construction & Building Materials

Marble, granite, ceramics, sanitary fittings, and electrical fixtures — bulk freight and LCL consolidation services.

Chemicals & Industrial Raw Materials

Hazardous and non-hazardous chemicals, plastics, and industrial raw materials with MSDS, ADR, and IMDG compliance documentation.

Sector 04

Integrated Logistics Services

One partner for every link in your international supply chain. Ocean, air, road, customs, warehousing — we cover it all, seamlessly.

Ocean Freight

FCL & LCL

Full Container Load and Less-than-Container Load services on the India–Europe–Middle East trade lanes. Competitive rates, reliable schedules, and port-to-port or door-to-door options.

Air Freight

Express & General Air

IATA-accredited air freight for time-critical shipments — medical devices, pharmaceuticals, high-value goods, and perishables. Hub airports: Mumbai, Delhi, London Heathrow, Milan Malpensa.

Customs Clearance

India, UK & EU Customs

Licensed customs brokers in India, United Kingdom, and Italy. We handle import/export declarations, HS code classification, duty calculations, and AEO/OEA certified handling.

Warehousing

Storage & Fulfilment

Bonded and non-bonded warehousing across Mumbai, London, Milan, and Dubai. Pick-and-pack, inventory management, and last-mile distribution for B2B buyers.

Trade Finance Support

LC & Documentary Credit

Guidance on Letters of Credit, Bank Guarantees, and EXIM Bank financing. We liaise with banking partners to ensure smooth payment flows in international trade transactions.

Digital Visibility

Shipment Tracking

Real-time cargo tracking, document management portal, and milestone notifications — keeping you informed at every step from factory to final delivery.
