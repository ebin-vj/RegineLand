Our History

From Mumbai to Milan
and Beyond

RegineLand Group began as a focused trade facilitation company connecting Indian manufacturers with European buyers — specifically in the healthcare and agri-food sectors where compliance and trust are non-negotiable.

Starting with a small but highly experienced team of trade specialists in Mumbai, we built our reputation shipment by shipment — getting the documentation right, keeping deadlines, and solving problems before they reached our clients.

As trust grew, so did our network. We established our first European office in Milan — a strategic gateway to the Italian and Mediterranean markets. A London presence followed, giving us deep access to the UK market and Heathrow-based air freight capabilities.

Today, RegineLand Group operates across four countries with a team of over 80 trade, logistics, and compliance specialists — handling thousands of shipments annually across three sectors.

The Beginning

RegineLand Group Founded

Established in Mumbai with a core team of trade finance and logistics specialists, focused on India–Italy agri-food exports.

Phase 1: Expansion

Healthcare Sector Launch

Secured first medical device export contracts with CE-certification support. Established relationships with ISO 13485 certified Indian manufacturers.

Phase 2: Europe

Milan Office Opens

First European office established in Milan — creating direct market access to Italy and Southern Europe. Port agency desk added in Genoa.

Phase 3: UK Market

London Presence Established

UK entity incorporated following Brexit to maintain frictionless customs clearance operations. Heathrow air freight hub activated.

Phase 4: Middle East

Dubai Hub Opened

DMCC-registered Dubai office serves as a re-export and transshipment gateway connecting India with the GCC, Africa, and global markets.

Present

Full Multinational Operations

80+ specialists, 6 offices, 40+ countries served. Continuing to grow with a focus on digital trade tools and sustainability-conscious logistics.
